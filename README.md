This repository relates to the course that will be taught at a private company. It specifically deals with working with Google Earth Engine (GEE) through R. Basics are explained such as connecting to the GEE API, linking to images, image collections, features and feature collections located on GEE. Uploading own feature collections to GEE is also explained. Furthermore, various types of filters are addressed connected to image and feature collections. Selecting image bands is also discused alongside getting RGB composites and computing spectral indices and getting the results to the local disks. Several aspects will be included in 40 bonus scripts. The material is currenly under development and will be offered to students of a future course whose websites will be published at https://www.datova-akademie.cz/kurzy. The language of the course is Czech, and, therefore, also the language in the scripts is Czech (with UTF-8 encoding).
